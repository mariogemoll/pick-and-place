Run a policy

The simulator and the policy both run here, in this tab. MuJoCo steps the same compiled scene the evaluation harness steps, and the state flow policy runs from its own checkpoint weights. Drag the cube and the black target plate anywhere in the workspace and press run: nothing about the episode you get was decided in advance.

This is a way to watch the policy, not a way to measure it. The engine is a different build with different rounding, and one episode is one episode. The scored results come from the Python harness over frozen scenario manifests.
